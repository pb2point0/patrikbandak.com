Healing Journey Wellness

Rebuilding the patient experience and the systems behind it.

I modernized Healing Journey Acupuncture & Wellness across the patient experience, core systems, workflows, and digital operations. The work included rebuilding the website and customer journey, migrating the practice from Unified Practice to Jane EHR, standardizing Google Workspace across devices, and connecting insurance remittances and payments directly into the EHR.

Overview

A patient experience problem that exposed a broader operating problem.

The inherited website contained useful information and years of credibility, but it asked prospective patients to do too much work. Important answers were buried in long pages, services were broadly framed, and the path from discovery to booking was inconsistent.

Healing Journey operates within a deliberate capacity model, so the objective was never growth at any cost. The work was about making the practice easier to understand, easier to access, and easier to operate: helping the right patients determine fit and take the appropriate next step, while simplifying the systems and workflows supporting that experience.

The challenge

The website was the visible part of a wider set of friction.

The original WordPress site reflected years of incremental additions. Behind it, day-to-day work also depended on aging systems and workflows that had evolved independently. The patient-facing experience and the operating environment both worked, but neither was as clear or connected as it could be.

  • Broad positioning made local relevance less immediate.
  • Long, information-dense pages competed with the primary patient journey.
  • Booking and insurance actions lacked consistent priority.
  • The visual system no longer reflected the current practice experience.
  • Administrative work depended too heavily on specific devices and disconnected tools.
  • EHR, insurance, and payment workflows had room to become simpler and more integrated.

Implementation

Working from the business outward.

On the patient-facing side, I audited the inherited content, navigation, booking paths, and search footprint, then mapped the customer journey and rebuilt the site around it. In parallel, I migrated the practice from Unified Practice to Jane EHR, standardized Google Workspace across three devices, and connected the EHR to electronic insurance remittances and direct payments.

The common thread was reducing unnecessary friction. Information needed to be easier to find, systems needed to work together more cleanly, and routine work needed to happen without depending on a particular device or workaround.

What the data showed

A stronger experience, with a more focused search footprint.

Not every top-line search metric increased. Overall Google impressions and clicks declined as broad informational visibility contracted, particularly around a legacy cosmetic-acupuncture page. At the same time, the remaining search visibility produced a higher click-through rate, average position improved, priority local-query performance strengthened, and organic visitors engaged more deeply with the site.

That distinction matters for a capacity-constrained care practice. Maximum reach was never the objective. Relevance, trust, and an efficient path to the right next step were.

The result

A practice that is easier to find, easier to navigate, and easier to run.

Healing Journey now has a more coherent experience from first search through booking, insurance, payment, and ongoing administration. The visible redesign was one part of a broader effort to connect the systems, workflows, and decisions behind the practice.
